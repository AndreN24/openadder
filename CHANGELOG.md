# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- Remap all 10 buttons of the Razer DeathAdder V2: key combinations (recorded), mouse buttons, media keys,
  DPI stage up/down/cycle, sniper DPI, profile switching, and starting a program or script.
- DPI stages (1–5, 100–20000 DPI) and polling rate, saved on the mouse.
- Logo and scroll-wheel lighting (static, breathing, spectrum, reactive, off) and brightness, saved on the mouse.
- Profiles: new, duplicate, rename, delete, reset; switch from the window, the tray, or a mouse button.
- Emergency shortcut Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Esc: all buttons back to normal, from anywhere.
- Runs in the tray, starts with Windows if you want, connects again by itself after you replug the mouse.
- No Razer Synapse, no account, no internet. About 19 MB of memory.
